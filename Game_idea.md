Shadow Tactics: Candlelight Skirmish

Shadow Tactics: Candlelight Skirmish is a turn-based tactical game where light and shadow factions battle for control of the battlefield. The game incorporates unique mechanics that leverage the interplay between light and darkness.

How to Play
Controls: Click to select units and hexes. Use the ability buttons (Move, Attack, Special) to perform actions.
Scoring System: Score points by controlling the red objective markers on the map. The first faction to reach 10 points wins!
Light Mechanics: Candles on the board create illuminated areas that boost Light faction units (+1 attack) but weaken Shadow faction units.
Dark Mechanics: Areas away from candle light benefit Shadow faction units (+1 attack) and hinder Light faction units.
Units and Special Abilities
Light Faction:

Luminary (Warrior): +1 attack when near candles
Sunbeam Archer: Can redirect candle light to new locations
Illuminator (Mage): Can create temporary light sources
Shadow Faction:

Umbral Knight (Warrior): +1 attack when in darkness
Void Stalker (Assassin): Can temporarily extinguish candles
Darkness Weaver (Mage): Creates areas of darkness
The game simulates a tabletop skirmish experience where positioning around light sources is a key tactical element, just as it would be in a real-world game played with candles.
